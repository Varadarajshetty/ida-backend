HR Policy - Acme Inc
====================

1. Leave policy
Employees may apply for leave through the HR Portal:
- Navigate to Dashboard -> Leave Request
- Select leave type (Casual, Medical, Earned)
- Attach supporting documents if required
Approval:
- All leave requests require approval from the employee's reporting manager.
- Medical leaves longer than 3 days require HR verification.

2. Attendance
- Core hours: 10:00 - 17:00
- Flexible start: employees may choose start time between 8:30 - 10:30.
- Late entries must be reported to HR if exceeding 30 minutes.

3. Remote Work
- Employees can request remote work for up to 2 days per week.
- Manager approval required; remote work depends on role suitability.

4. Onboarding & Offboarding
- New hires will complete compliance training within first week.
- Exit clearances: IT, HR, Finance must sign off.
